## What I do

**Infrastructure as code.** Multi-AZ AWS platforms in Terraform — VPC, ALB, Auto Scaling, EC2 launch
templates, RDS Multi-AZ, Route 53, ACM, ECR, Secrets Manager, SSM — replacing hand-built single
instances with environments that survive an AZ failure and take zero-downtime releases.

**Delivery pipelines.** CI/CD standardized across 5+ products on GitHub Actions and GitLab CI:
build, test, OWASP-aligned scanning with SonarQube and Snyk, push to ECR, rolling deploy. Cut change
failure rate by 30%.

**Observability and on-call.** Prometheus, Grafana, Node Exporter, Blackbox Exporter, CloudWatch and
SNS, alerting on ASG lifecycle events, RDS saturation and endpoint health — with the runbook that
makes the alert actionable at 3am.

**Cost.** 40% off a monthly AWS bill through right-sizing, Auto Scaling target tracking, caching to
take load off RDS, and deleting idle capacity.
